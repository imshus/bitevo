Our Story

Why we started BiteVo

We've all had that moment: you're hungry, you order something, and what arrives is cold, late or just not what you hoped for. We started BiteVo to change that.

Our idea was simple. Cook every dish fresh in our own kitchens, use ingredients we would happily serve our own families, and get it to your door while it's still hot. No shortcuts and no compromises on taste.

That's where our name and our promise come from: good food, better mood. Every order we send out should make your day a little better.

Our Mission

To make fresh, tasty and hygienic food easy to order, so a great meal is always just a few taps away.

Our Promise

Every dish cooked fresh, packed with care and delivered hot. If something isn't right, we'll make it right.
