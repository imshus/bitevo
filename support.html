FAQs

Common questions

Where is my order?

You can follow your order live in the BiteVo app. If it's taking longer than expected, call us on +91 93193 46656 with your order ID and we'll check right away.

Something is wrong or missing in my order

We're sorry about that! Please contact us as soon as possible with your order ID and, if you can, a photo of the issue. We'll make it right.

Can I cancel my order?

Call us as soon as you can. Since we start cooking quickly after an order is placed, the sooner you reach us, the easier it is for us to help.

When will I get my refund?

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. The time it takes to reflect depends on your bank or payment provider.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All the payment options available to you are shown at checkout in the BiteVo app.

Do you deliver to my area?

Enter your delivery address in the BiteVo app to see whether we currently deliver to you.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Email us at info@bitevo.com from your registered email, or call us, and we'll take care of it. See our Privacy Policy for more.
